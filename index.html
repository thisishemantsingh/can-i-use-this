    This is educational screening, not legal advice

    “Can I Use This?” provides simplified, rule-based information and risk indicators only. It does not provide legal advice, does not guarantee that any reuse is lawful, and does not replace consultation with a qualified copyright lawyer. Fair dealing and fair use are decided case by case — a low-risk signal here is not a clearance.
